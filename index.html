What is a Health Card?

A Health Card is a card issued by institutions such as schools, colleges, and hospitals through which a person can receive free or discounted medical services. Health cards are used worldwide to streamline access to healthcare.

Information Contained in a Health Card

  • Name
  • Date of Issuing / Date of Admission
  • Contact Number
  • Registration Number
  • Photo of the person
  • Institution Name

About Health Card Generator

Health Card Generator is a desktop software that allows you to create digital or printable health cards. It automatically includes Registration Number, Name, Contact Number, Date of Admission (DOA), and Blood Group in the generated card.

Using this software, you can easily generate and print a health card for personal or institutional use.

How to Use

  1. Download and install the software.
  2. Open the application and enter your information.
  3. Click the “Enter” button to preview both sides of the health card.
  4. Select a format (PDF, XPS) and save it to your computer.
  5. Print the card and use it for medical services.
